# Business Case

## Problem

Ministries process large volumes of environmental applications, supporting studies, correspondence, inspection materials, and compliance records. Manual triage, document validation, regulatory mapping, and evidence review create delays, inconsistent decisions, and limited auditability.

## Proposed Solution

GoAnalyze Government provides a secure AI document intelligence platform that ingests ministry records, extracts metadata, indexes content, maps evidence to regulatory obligations, detects missing documents, scores case risk, and routes work through auditable review queues.

## Strategic Outcomes

- Faster admissibility screening and document completeness checks.
- Better consistency in environmental authorization review.
- Reduced analyst time spent on document search and summarization.
- Stronger transparency through citations, confidence scores, and audit trails.
- Multi-ministry reuse through tenant-aware architecture.

## Measurable Benefits

| Benefit | Measurement |
|---|---|
| Intake cycle time reduction | Median days from submission to admissibility decision |
| Analyst productivity | Documents reviewed per analyst per day |
| Decision defensibility | Percentage of AI recommendations with citations and human review disposition |
| SLA performance | Queue-level SLA compliance and escalation volume |
| Compliance posture | Audit events captured per sensitive action |
