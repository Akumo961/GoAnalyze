# Procurement Positioning

## Positioning Statement

GoAnalyze Government is an open, ministry-configurable AI document intelligence platform focused on defensible regulatory review, evidence tracing, and public-sector control over data, models, workflows, and deployment topology.

## Differentiators

- Environmental authorization review engine with admissibility, risk, missing-document, compliance, and justification outputs.
- Explainable AI by design: citations, confidence, groundedness, and human review workflows.
- Government-controlled deployment using Kubernetes, Helm, Terraform, Keycloak, PostgreSQL, OpenSearch, MinIO, Kafka, and Temporal.
- Multi-tenant architecture for ministry reuse without vendor lock-in.
- Audit-first model with hash-chained audit events and purpose-based access controls.

## Procurement Strategy

- Position as a configurable platform and accelerator, not a closed SaaS document repository.
- Use modular procurement lots for platform hardening, integration, AI governance, workflow implementation, and managed operations.
- Require open APIs, exportable data, model governance evidence, and operational observability as acceptance criteria.
